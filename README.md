## Research

The common thread in my work is models that still recognise the right thing when real-world conditions change.

* **Robust visual recognition:** networks that hold up under new viewing angles, lighting and imaging modalities, from indoor drone navigation to medical imaging.
* **Security and trust:** anti-counterfeiting, network intrusion detection, and the safety and alignment of large language models.
* **Machine learning and optimization for physical systems:** AI for materials, wildfire risk mapping, and a non-linear drug-delivery controller for cancer treatment that combines SMC, ISMC and synergetic controllers tuned with a two-phase genetic algorithm.
